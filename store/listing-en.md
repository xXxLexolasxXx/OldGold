## Full description
Build a gnome village from scratch — and keep it alive.

Your gnomes don't stand around waiting for orders. They haul carts along the roads you lay, chop, forge, bake, brew — and take their smoke breaks. Lay a road and goods start moving. Forget one and the smithy sits idle while the smith sulks.

**Features**
- **A living economy:** farms, apiaries, breweries, mines, workshops, storehouses, stables. Chains from grain to armour and from stone to magic staves.
- **Brick-by-brick construction:** buildings grow before your eyes; rotate the entrance.
- **Gnomes with personality:** smoke breaks, fatigue, festivals and clean-up days, a drunken nap in the middle of the road (yes, it blocks traffic), injuries, habits.
- **A hero to level up:** gear, spells, hunts in the forest against fairies, leshies and boars. Cartoon fantasy combat, no blood.
- **Day and night:** ghosts and skeletons come out after dusk — build a graveyard, light the lanterns.
- **Goals and story:** Settlement → Village → Town milestones and a 6-chapter campaign.
- **Co-op:** share one village with friends on a server.
- **Own pixel style:** all art and music are original and made for this game.
- **Mobile-ready:** touch controls, works offline (except co-op).

**Content note:** cartoon fantasy violence; gnomes smoke pipes and drink ale (no depiction of real-world harm).

##### js/changelog.js
New reaper horns
Reaper horns: the ram ones curl tighter; the second kind is a single horn wriggling upward like a snake; the third is a single horn spiralling over the head
Tutorial updated
The tutorial got shorter (12 steps instead of 15) and goes in order: village and road → bread and estate → night → hero → combat and mana → guilds → "you're on your own"
Neutrals, fairies, mimics, the festival, Cleanup Day, co-op and the goat with the carter get short collapsible "Learn more" blurbs, no overload
The window header shows the tutorial section; on phone and PC the window folds into a single line so it doesn't cover the map
The tutorial no longer starts under the start menu and the "What's your hero's name?" window
Removed the confusing steps: the "come here" order is merged with hero work; dash, rally cry and sidekick moved into the combat step
Strange night visitors
Five strange creatures nothing like animals: Goo (from night 5), Lampskull (from 7), Sentinel (from 10), Ribbon (from 12) and the Shard Round-Dance (from 15)
Each night a wild beast has a 15–20% chance to be swapped for one of the unlocked creatures, and returns at dawn; on the night a new kind unlocks, it always shows up
Each has its own frames: Goo hops (6), Lampskull steps and blinks (6), Sentinel's gears tick (8), Ribbon writhes (8), Shards swirl (8)
Menagerie
Boar roams the forest rooting for acorns and mushrooms; charges and bites a lone gnome (limp), backs off from the hero and three gnomes, and can be hunted by the hero: hides and meat
Bear reaches for beehives for honey, fears no bees and is dangerous to anyone who comes near; only four together can drive it off, it mauls the hero, but the hero can beat it
Four cats (ginger, grey tabby, black, white-spotted) settle near the storehouse and bakery, catch mice, can be tamed with bread and purr
Storks nest on a tall roof by the water and haul fish from the lake; the heron stands in the shallows among reeds and steals the anglers' catch (the hero or three gnomes chase it off)
Fish are visible in the lakes: a shadow under the water, splashes and a flash when one jumps
Farms get beehives with bees: gnomes collect honey in passing, and the bear steals it
A big flock of crows circles over a ripe field and pecks the harvest; a scarecrow, the hero or three gnomes scare them off
Fairy-tale creatures
A brownie behind the oven of the bakery and mill: in the evening the baker sets out a saucer of milk, and at night the brownie fixes the building and bakes bread or grinds flour itself; forget the saucer and it hides a tool and drops a load. Rarely seen: eyes glow at night and a silhouette flickers by every few minutes
The unicorn comes to the spring on a clear evening and heals the wounded, but gets spooked if you come too close
A mountain dragonling hauls gold from piles and the storehouse by day and sometimes sets a field on fire; the hero can chase it off or tame it — then it carries gold for you
A griffin patrols the mountains and guards a treasure: it scares gnomes, while the hero and recruits can beat it and take the hoard
A fire salamander by the smelter and armory: when they're stoking, it glows brighter and work goes a little faster
A goldfish in the lake (the hero catches it — get a ransom in gold) and a golden goose among the geese: once a day it lays a golden egg, if the flock isn't bullied
Dark tales
A kikimora in the reeds by the water: grabs a gnome from the shore and drags them into the mud along with the load. Pay-off — food left on a hummock
The leshy, master of the forest: if too many trees get felled in a day, he leads the woodcutters in circles. Bread on a stump softens him up
A water sprite and a mermaid in the lake: the sprite drags loads off the shore, the mermaid sings and lures gnomes to the water. Beer into the water — and both go quiet
A banshee wails at the graveyard and before a gnome's doom (log entry, blinking icon), scaring nearby workers
A ghostly wolf follows the night fiends, howls and steals lanterns. A bonfire and the hero drive it off, the lantern comes back
Festival!
New event — "Festival": every few days, via a button in the tavern or after beating the fiends, all gnomes drop their work, head to the tavern, drink ale, dance and shout "hooray". Lasts a day or two
Garlands with flags between the lanterns and on the tavern, barrels by the door, confetti, clinking mugs
Ale and food go faster, and after the festival gnomes suffer hangovers and move sluggishly
Fiends and the coming of evening break up the party; at night it quiets down, and a multi-day festival resumes in the morning
Horned reapers and sickle claws
The reaper now wears a black horned helmet; the horns are demonic, in three shapes: curled ram, tall bull, a crown of spikes (each reaper has its own)
Fingers of ghosts and the reaper: jointed phalanges and sickle claws, curved and sharp
New event — "Festival": every few days (via a button in the tavern, or after beating the fiends) all gnomes drop their work, head to the tavern, drink ale, dance and shout "hooray". Lasts a day or two
Garlands with flags between the lanterns and on the tavern, barrels by the door, confetti; clinking mugs and a general hum
Fiends and the coming of evening break up the party
Look of the undead
The reaper now looks like Death: a roomy black hood with a skull in the shadow and a long scythe with a sickle blade
Ghost hands got smaller, with longer and thinner claws
Ghosts and the reaper
Ghosts were redrawn from scratch: bodiless paws with claws, a sinister hood with bone horns, a grainy tail, grave eyes (red only when scaring)
New tier-2 undead — the reaper ghost: from night 3, on the outskirts, one at a time. Takes no bread, can't be banished by prayer, snuffs lanterns, scares the whole neighbourhood and scythes the hero
Ghosts have 9 hover frames and 8 strike frames: if the hero banishes a ghost, it snaps back with claws
Ghosts no longer drift off the edge of the map
A ghost soothed with bread stopped scaring gnomes
Ghosts dissolve when dawn begins, not at the very end; loading at dawn doesn't summon new fiends

Basics
A big stone barn. Stores all goods; haulers bring the surplus here.
School
A two-storey school with a bell tower. Trains gnomes for 1 gold.
Tavern
Hungry gnomes eat chicken, sausage or bread here and wash it down with beer. A chicken carcass is cut into 4 pieces. A drunk may fall asleep right on the road.
Big bonfire
Resources
Haulers carry trash here from the bins. Burning, it makes ash — farms use it to fertilize fields, so crops grow faster and richer.
Goat pen
The herder tames wild goats. A tame goat hauls goods along roads — 2 at a time. Up to 3 goats per pen.
The stableman raises horses on hay and grain. Each horse with a cart is a carter: hauls a whole stack at once, up to 5 items. Up to 3 per stable.
Woodcutter's house
A log cabin. The woodcutter fells mature trees and plants new ones.
Sawmill
Saws logs into planks.
Quarry
Mines stone. Build next to boulders.
Gold mine
Mines gold ore. Build flush against a cliff.
Smelter
Smelts ore into gold, burning logs.
Iron mine
Mines iron ore. Build flush against a cliff.
Metal smelter
A bloomery with bellows: iron ore and logs become iron ingots for weapons and armor.
Armory
The smith-armorer forges swords and daggers, bends bows. The hero and recruits take weapons here.
Farm
The farmer sows and reaps the wheat fields you marked nearby. Hay is a by-product of harvest.
Hop yard
The hop-grower raises hops on poles around and dries the cones in the tower.
Brewery
Brews beer from grain and hops. Beer goes to the tavern.
Mill
Grinds grain into flour.
Bakery
Bakes bread from flour.
Pigsty
Every armful of hay: the pigs grow a third bigger and a piglet appears. Adults go to the slaughterhouse.
Slaughterhouse
A pig becomes sausage and a hide.
Tannery
Cures hides into leather.
Armor workshop
Sews leather armor, and with iron on hand rivets iron armor too.
Magic stone mine
The miner digs magic stones. Build under a cliff with crystals.
Magic circle
Stones give their power to mana jars and turn into plain stones. Works on its own.
Magic workshop
The sorcerer makes staves and mage robes.
Warriors' guild
A stone hall with a tower and a training yard. The Warrior Master teaches how to hold a sword and wear iron.
Archers' guild
A forest lodge with a watchtower and targets. The Archery Master teaches marksmanship and quick feet.
Rogues' guild
A lopsided house with a cellar and blind windows. The Gang Boss teaches sleight of hand and the finer points of what's lying around.
Mages' guild
A tower with a starry dome and a library. The Archmage teaches wits and spells.

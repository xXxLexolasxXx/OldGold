##### js/changelog.js#2
Guilds grow, a fair and the Barrow Prince
Guilds: fame and titles for the guildmaster — Novices, Journeymen, Masters, Legend (stars above the master), each rank with its own bonus. The guild window has the "Guild Path": what's done, the next step and what it gives
Recruit branches from level 5 ("veteran" from 10): Guardian or Hacker, Sniper or Tracker, Shade or Pickpocket, Pyromancer or Healer — chosen on the recruit's card
A daily guild quest ("Night Watch", "Hunt", "Drills", "Study") and the "Village Cup" every three days: the guild with the most fame gets the cup and +20% XP for its recruits
Guild tier 3 (needs tier 2 and the "Masters" rank): a golden crown above the hall, recruits up to level 18, and the hero can become a grandmaster (rank 6)
A fair every 5–7 days on the square by the storehouse: five stalls, trading with merchants, contests (hammer, target, riddles, pies) — the hero against gnomes and recruits, prizes and the "Fair Champion" title. After a good fair — a festival
Co-op: at the fair players swap items from their backpacks (lots), and on fair day there's a personal quest "Play in a contest"
The Barrow Prince — a boss for two or more heroes (from level 3; solo — the hero and two "Follow hero" recruits): blue lights over the barrow at night, three phases — bone wards, grave chill, rage. Archers break the wards, fighters hold the prince, the rogue strikes from behind. Rewards and titles: "Prince Slayer", "Squad Blade", "Squad Shield", "Ward Breaker"
Different maps: biomes, rivers and blizzards
New game → after the mode, pick a map: meadows, deep forest, steppe, mountains, swamp or winter (snow, ice on the water, blizzards); size small 96×72 / medium 128×96 / large 160×120; your own seed (empty — random)
Rivers with fords, irregular lakes, mountain ridges with passes and veins of gold and iron; more crystals in the mountains and winter
Own trees, rocks and mountains for each biome: snowy firs, yellow steppe, dark thicket, reeds in the swamp; in winter snow falls instead of rain
Terrain events: blizzard (winter, mountains) and flood (meadows, forest, steppe, swamp) — water floods the floodplains and fords, gnomes slip
Biome and size are stored in the save; old saves load as before. In co-op the server builds the map from the seed (variables OLDGOLD_BIOME, OLDGOLD_SIZE, OLDGOLD_SEED), clients draw it themselves
Trade and caravans
New building "Trading Post" ("Basics" tab, unlocked after the bakery). Caravans arrive along the road from beyond the map edge: a bread wagon, a wandering peddler, later iron and sorcerer ones
The "Trade" tab (the purse on the panel): a merchant sells and buys goods for gold from the storehouses. Prices float: they rise when you buy, fall when you sell, and drift back to normal. There are rare goods and a small purse for each merchant
Events: robbers on the road (send the hero or guild fighters to the wagon — otherwise the merchant arrives robbed), rare goods, price shifts ("crop failure", "war rumours", "construction")
Turnover grows: Shop → Trading Post → Market → … — caravans come more often and richer. At the fair a caravan arrives at once and on better terms; during Malgor's raids they come less often
The caravan is visible on the map (wagon, mule, merchant). Works on a phone too; in co-op the server counts everything, everyone sees it. Saved along with the game
No more lag
The main menu no longer stutters: the bonfire backdrop draws 6–10 times faster (on a weak phone — smooth instead of jerky)
The game opens faster: to the menu on PC ~1 s instead of 4, on a weak phone ~8 s instead of almost half a minute — the ground finishes drawing while you're in the menu
"Continue" without a frozen screen: a big village loads with a percentage, and the first seconds of a new game run smoothly
Gnomes on the road stand idle less: oncoming ones on a narrow road no longer lock horns for 3 seconds, delivery is faster
Pathfinding ~9 times faster: beasts across the river no longer hang the game
Piles on the ground and loads in hands no longer vanish when saving
A scare during a smoke break and similar cases no longer lose a delivery; goods don't stay "busy" forever
A builder doesn't stand forever at a site with no material — goes to another one
The owl no longer carries off elk and wolves (only rabbits, chickens and ducks) and doesn't bring trash with nowhere to put it
The dark mage Malgor
The fiends got a master: the dark mage Malgor lives in the Black Spire — that very tower from the intro — at the far edge of the map
His wrath grows every night and with the village's size: green windows and watch fires → scout crows → curses (lanterns go out, famine plague, extra skeletons) → raids → a siege with dark knights
Minions come out of the fog at night, beat gnomes and haul goods from the storehouse; they can't stand against the big bonfire, at dawn they leave and drop their loot
The march to the spire: Malgor fights in three phases — summoning servants, green fire under the guardian knight's shield, rage with teleports. Victory puts out the spire for good and gives gold, magic stones and a title
The spire is on the minimap, wrath and stage are on the hero card; new achievements. In co-op the server counts everything, the fight with Malgor is shared
Help for newcomers
In the building window — a red "why it's stuck" plate with a reason and action: no road, no worker, missing goods (and where to get them), all full, paused, damaged. Tap a building on a phone — the reason pops up at once
The "Estate" tab: every problem has a hint on what to do, and there's a "Balance per minute" — what's produced and spent per resource, how much is in the storehouse, where the shortage is
Help (menu button or the H key): buildings, resources, creatures, hero, nights, controls — short sarcastic cards with search by name. From the building window — "What building is this?"
First-time hints, one at a time and once: first hunger, evening, first loss, ghost, tier-2 building, the icon above a stalled building, phone gestures. They don't cover the map and go away by themselves
New game: choose "Scenario" or "Free play". Goals panel at the top left (collapsible)
Difficulty: Calm, Normal, Survival
"New game" now has a difficulty choice. "Calm": nights twice as gentle and shorter, fiends weaker, gnomes don't die or collapse from hunger, more supplies at the start — just build and explore
"Normal" — the old balance, all old saves continue in it
"Survival": nights longer and meaner, more fiends, faster hunger, a poorer storehouse, the hero lies longer and gets up with a quarter health, and each gnome lost adds ghosts and skeletons
Forest neutrals depend on the mode too: in "Calm" they hit weaker and respawn slower, in "Survival" they're meaner and tougher
The mode shows in the "Menu" tab and on the "Continue" card; saved in the save. In co-op the host picks the village difficulty (the player-permissions window), the server counts it
Reaper's eyes
The reaper's helmet has a wide cut-out with a frowning brow: the angry eyes are clearly visible
Dark tales: new faces
The banshee is now like a ghost, only a woman: long hair, a hem crumbling into haze, light hands with tiny claws
The ghostly wolf — thin, hunched, snarling, with raised hackles and visible ribs
The water sprite got toothy: bulging eyes, a mouth with a saw of teeth, a beard of slime
New: will-o'-the-wisps lead a gnome without a lantern into the mud, and the Noon Witch in the heat addles a peasant's head in the field (beer drives her off)
The kikimora, mermaid and water sprite no longer vanish at the very start of the evening
Dragonling, dragon and slim fish
A baby dragonling appeared: steals 1–3 gold, and after six raids grows into a big dragon (3–6 gold, sets fields on fire more often)
Slim fish of new colours splash in the lakes — amber, pink, turquoise: they leap from the water with spray
The brownie Kuzya looks like a human now: pink skin, ginger beard, a shirt with a patch
The fire salamander got big and recognizable: black with yellow spots, a broad head, a smouldering tail
Lanky now carries off big trees with their roots
A smaller reaper helmet
The reaper's helmet got smaller, the horns stayed the same size
Fairy-tale creatures in detail
The unicorn, dragonling, griffin, brownie Kuzya and fire salamander were redrawn: more detail, joints, smooth shapes and outlines
Fish in the lake come in three colours — gold, red and blue: the hero who catches one gets a ransom (gold, mana jars or magic stones)
Lanky — a dark neutral silhouette with purple eyes: roams the forests, digs up trees and carries them off (sometimes plants them elsewhere); if you come near — it vanishes
Crescent on legs
A new night creature from night 18: a crescent moon with eyes on legs (steps, blinks)
The sentinel got eyes: they squint and blink
The lampskull was redone: a street lantern with a handle and a flame on a pole with twig arms and a scarf

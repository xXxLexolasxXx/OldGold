We've Arrived
Chapter 1. We've Arrived
The wagons creaked and stopped. Ahead: a clearing, a forest and not a living soul. If you don't count the gnomes.
The land is free. Suspiciously free. That usually happens when nobody wants it.
Far above the forest, a tower sticks out of a cliff. Its windows glow a poisonous green.
That's probably a lighthouse. Or a neighbour. Let's start with firewood and see.
A woodcutter's house, a quarry, a sawmill — and survive till morning. At night the forest isn't as simple as by day.
Someone is chopping trees at the foot of my tower. I'll remember that. I remember everything, but slowly.
Bread and Distrust
Chapter 2. Bread and Distrust
A gnome without bread is just a small angry man with a beard. Let's build a farm, a mill and a bakery.
Noise. Noise again. Who allowed hammering in my forest?
The forest isn't his, but arguing with a man who owns a tower feels somehow awkward.
Farm → mill → bakery. Forty loaves of bread and fourteen gnomes.
Beer Instead of Conscience
Chapter 3. Beer Instead of Conscience
A scroll lay by the gate in the morning. Heraldic paper, green ink, a whiff of sulphur.
INVOICE for breach of silence: 40 gold and your conscience. The conscience may be paid in installments.
No gold, and the conscience was drunk away long ago. So, beer. We need hops and a brewer.
A hop yard, a brewery, twenty-five mugs — and survive three nights.
A Pig Is a Gnome's Friend
Chapter 4. A Pig Is a Gnome's Friend
Sausage. Leather. Armor. In exactly that order, because sausage is tastier.
You breed pigs. In my tower I have spiders. We are different people.
A pigsty, a slaughterhouse, a tannery, an armor workshop. The fifth night is a long one.
The fifth night is behind us, and we're still here. That's almost an achievement. Almost.
Shine and Iron
Chapter 5. Shine and Iron
Gold is beautiful. Iron is useful. The hero needs the second, the gnomes the first. As usual.
The tower on the cliff blinked its windows. It seems to have looked at the village.
Well, well. Guilds, iron, a hero… And my magic stones in your storehouse. Curious. No, I'm not curious. I don't like it.

Chapter 6. Silence Is Golden
I asked for silence and my stones back. You brought noise and forgot the stones. The nights are mine now.
He means it. I'd be scared, but I'm old and lazy.
A hero, guilds, bonfires and bread. All we've got. We'll manage.
A dark mage in the Black Spire. Gather the hero, guilds and supplies, go to the spire and defeat Malgor.
Epilogue
The tower went dark all at once, like a candle that was finally noticed. The forest grew quiet.

Building window
Goal:
grow your settlement. Builders build, haulers haul, and everything is, of course, your fault.
On a phone:
one finger pans the map, two zoom. The tabs at the bottom open the sheet. Pick a building, tap a spot to see a preview, tap again or hit ✓. Draw roads with your finger.
Pick a building on the left and click the map. A bit of road appears in front of the door; connect it to the road network.
Haulers walk on roads only.
Not one step on grass, they have principles. A building with no road to the storehouse gets no deliveries (the icon above it will remind you at length and with reproach).
— drag with the mouse, a tile costs 1 stone. Start new roads from an existing one: a road from nowhere to nowhere isn't a road, it's landscape design.
A road two tiles wide has two lanes: haulers pass without slowing down. On a narrow one they squeeze past each other slowly and give you a look of silent reproach.
Workers train at the
school
for gold. The "Train" button in the building window queues the right one. Haulers and builders eventually
level up to tier 2 and 3
: they carry 2–3 items and fetch building materials themselves. Watch the patches on their caps and the bars in the "Gnomes" tab.
Food:
farm (wheat) → mill → bakery
or
hay (a by-product of harvest) → pigsty → slaughterhouse
(sausage). You mark the fields yourself with the "Wheat" and "Hops" tools next to the farm.
Pigsty: every armful of hay makes the pigs a third bigger and a piglet is born; adults go to the slaughterhouse.
Leather: the slaughterhouse yields hides →
tannery
→ leather →
armor workshop

Guilds
of warriors, archers, rogues and mages: the guildmaster teaches the hero a class (click a guild with the hero) and recruits from the haulers. Click a recruit to select, the ground to set a post, an enemy to attack. Hero attack —
Icons above buildings tell you what's missing. The "Estate" tab gathers all the problems into one list, so you can be upset more conveniently.

Hero and combat
Click a guild with the hero — they learn a class. Attack — Q. Click a recruit to select, the ground to set a post, an enemy to attack. Health bar on the left; the hero takes food from the tavern, mana from jars. If killed — respawns at the big bonfire.
Not immortal, just persistent.
Night and fiends
A day: day → evening (darkens, lanterns) → foggy night → dawn. Every 5th night is longer. Near the big bonfire (radius 8 tiles) fiends don't come and it's bright. A fallen gnome = a broken lantern, and they get lost in the fog. Losses grow the number of ghosts and skeletons.
Food and hunger
A hungry gnome (fullness below 30%) goes to the tavern. No food — they faint and die. Chains: farm → mill → bakery (bread); hay → pigsty → slaughterhouse (sausage); wheat + hops → brewery.
A hungry gnome is an angry gnome, but not for long.
Roads and deliveries
Haulers walk on roads only. If a building's entrance isn't connected to the storehouse, it stands idle. A road two tiles wide has two lanes. The "Deliveries" button in the building window forbids bringing goods there (B).
A road from nowhere to nowhere isn't a road, it's landscape design.
Tiers and upgrades
Haulers and builders grow up to tier 3 (patches on caps). Buildings upgrade to tier 2: the "Upgrade" button in the building window — haulers bring the materials, and production stands still while it's being built.
Everyone can grow. Except salaries.
